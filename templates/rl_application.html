Q-Learning in a 10 × 10 Environment

The agent starts at A and must reach T. It learns the route on its own through trial and error, with an SGDRegressor that estimates the value of each action.

4. Train the agent

Each episode starts at A. The agent chooses its actions with epsilon-greedy, and at the end of the episode partial_fit() updates the Q-values with every transition it observed. After training, the policy is evaluated without exploration.
