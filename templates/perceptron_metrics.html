Interpretation of the results

The Perceptron classifies correctly {{ "%.1f"|format(metrics.accuracy * 100) }}% of the campaigns it had never seen. The errors gather around campaigns whose combination of budget, duration and channels leaves them close to the break-even point, where the outcome depends on factors the model does not observe.

For this problem a false positive costs more than a false negative: approving a campaign that will not recover its investment spends real money, while rejecting a campaign that would have worked only means a missed opportunity. That makes precision the metric to watch, currently at {{ "%.1f"|format(metrics.precision * 100) }}%.
