Logistic Regression Application

Classifying whether a month reached its sales goal.

1. Problem context

A consumer goods company sets a monthly goal of {{ info.monthly_goal }} units for one of its products. Every month it invests a different amount in advertising, and the marketing team needs to know in advance whether that investment is enough to reach the goal, so the budget can be corrected before the month ends.

What is being analysed
Whether the advertising investment of a month is enough to reach the sales goal.
What the data represents
Each record is one month: the amount invested in advertising and whether the goal was reached.
Independent variable
{{ info.feature_name }}, in {{ info.feature_unit }}.
Target variable
{{ info.target_name }}, binary.
Possible classes
0 = {{ info.classes[0] }} · 1 = {{ info.classes[1] }}
What the model classifies
Given an advertising investment, whether that month belongs to the group that reaches the goal or to the group that misses it.

Each point is one month, placed at its advertising investment and coloured by its class. The dark curve is the probability of reaching the goal estimated by the model, and the dashed line is the 0.5 threshold that separates the two classes.
