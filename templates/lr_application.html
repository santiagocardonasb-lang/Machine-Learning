Linear Regression Application

Predicting product sales from advertising investment.

1. Problem context

A consumer goods company invests a different amount in advertising every month and needs to know how much of that spending turns into sales. Without a model, the budget is decided by intuition, and there is no way to justify raising or cutting it.

Phenomenon analysed
How monthly advertising investment affects the number of units sold of a product.
What the data represents
Each record is one month: how much was invested in advertising and how many units were sold that month.
Why Linear Regression
Both variables are quantitative and the relationship between them is approximately linear, which is exactly the situation the algorithm is designed for.
Value being predicted
The units sold expected for a given advertising investment.
